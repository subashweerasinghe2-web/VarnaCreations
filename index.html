FAQ

Good to know

How long does a custom design take?

Most designs are ready within 48–72 hours of confirming your details, depending on complexity and revisions.

Can I request changes after the first draft?

Yes — every order includes two rounds of revisions so the wording, colours, and layout feel exactly right.

What formats do I receive?

You'll receive a high-resolution image and an animated video version, ready to share on WhatsApp, Instagram, or email.

Do you offer printed invitations too?

Our focus is digital, but we're happy to prepare a print-ready file if you'd like physical copies as keepsakes.
